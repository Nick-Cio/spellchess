Basics

Chess, plus a hand of spells.

Mode one

Original

Fewer spells, bigger swings. One cast per turn, and a spell can win material outright.

How it starts

15-card deck, 3 of each. You get 3 and may swap 1. Two deck cards are revealed at the start, one more every 4 moves.

Mode two

Draft

Spells never take material and you may cast two per turn, so they're about moving pieces: tempo, blocking, slipping through, setting up combinations.

The draft

Three pairs, one at a time. Keep one, the other goes to your opponent. Then both hands are face up.
